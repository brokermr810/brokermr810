# Open Byte Inc.

### Building open-source, AI-native infrastructure for quantitative trading.

**Open Byte Inc.** is the organization behind **QuantDinger**, an open-source quantitative trading platform that connects market research, AI-assisted analysis, strategy development, backtesting, risk management, and live execution in one unified workflow.

We are committed to building transparent, extensible, and professional-grade financial technology for traders, developers, researchers, and quantitative teams across global markets.
